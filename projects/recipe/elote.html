Street-Style Elote

Ingredients

  • 4 ears of corn, husked
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1/2 cup crumbled cotija cheese
  • 1 tsp chili powder or Tajín
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Pinch of salt

Goes Well With

  • Carne asada
  • Chicken quesadillas
  • Tacos al pastor
  • Black bean salad
  • Agua fresca or horchata

Steps

  1. Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high. Brush the corn lightly with melted butter.
  2. Grill the corn for 8–10 minutes, turning every couple of minutes, until it's tender and charred in spots all the way around.
  3. While the corn cooks, stir together the mayo, crema, grated garlic, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl.
  4. Spread the cotija on a plate so it's easy to roll the corn in.
  5. While the corn is still hot, brush or spread each ear with the mayo mixture, then roll it in the cotija so it sticks.
  6. Dust with chili powder or Tajín, sprinkle with cilantro, and serve right away with lime wedges to squeeze over the top.

Smoky Grilled Carne Asada

Ingredients

  • 2 lb skirt or flank steak
  • Juice of 2 limes and 1 orange
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jalapeno, minced
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper

Plate Sides

  • Mexican rice
  • Refried or pinto beans
  • Warm corn tortillas
  • Pico de gallo
  • Sliced avocado or guac
  • Lime wedges

Steps

  1. Whisk all the marinade ingredients together, add the steak, and marinate in the fridge for 2 to 8 hours. Don't go much past 8 or the citrus starts making the texture mushy.
  2. Pull the steak out about 30 minutes before cooking so it isn't fridge-cold. Pat it mostly dry so it sears instead of steams.
  3. Get a grill or cast-iron pan very hot. Cook skirt steak about 3–4 minutes per side, flank about 5–6, aiming for 130–135°F for medium-rare to medium.
  4. Rest it 5-10 minutes, then slice thin against the grain. This matters most with skirt, which is chewy if cut the wrong way.
  5. While it rests, warm the tortillas on the grill or in a dry pan and make the pico.
  6. Plate the sliced steak with rice, beans, pico, avocado, and tortillas, with lime wedges to squeeze over everything.
